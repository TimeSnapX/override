HOW TO PLAY

Survive 10 waves of SYNOD machines on the Fortitude Heights rooftop. Wave 10 is the Warden.

Phone: left thumb = move (floating stick), drag right side = look, hold FIRE (you can drag it to aim too). Rail weapons charge while held, fire on release. ◎ = thermal scope.

Desktop: WASD · mouse · click fire · right-click scope · R reload · 1–5 / wheel / Q weapons · Shift dash · Space jump · E OVERRIDE / play cabinet · Esc pause · M mute.

OVERRIDE (🦩 meter): when full, hack the nearest machine — it fights for you for 10 s, then self-destructs.

Weak points: Wasp eyes, Sentinel cores (shoot the chest plates to pop them open), the Warden's core once its shield nodes are down.

Arcade corner (south-west, under the awning) is open before wave 1 and in breathers. Beat target scores for rewards.

Rumour has it there are three golden bananas somewhere on this roof…
